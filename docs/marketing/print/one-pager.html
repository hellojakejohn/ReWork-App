Real jobs, and a resume tailored to each one, with nothing made up.

A free-to-start tool your job seekers can use on a phone or a library computer. It finds open jobs that fit them, warns about ghost jobs and scams, and rewrites their resume for each job using only what is true about them.

Jobs that are really open

Tens of thousands of jobs pulled every day from employers' own job boards, not reposts. Jobs that leave the employer's board leave ReWork.

Ghost job and scam check

Paste any job link at rework.careers/check: is it still on the employer's board, how old is it, does it look like a scam. Free, no sign-up.

Honest tailoring

Every change comes with a receipt: what changed and why. It never adds a skill, title or number the person did not give. A counselor can check every line.

Built for career changers

It finds the words a job uses for what the person already did. In our tests, career changers' match rose from about 40% to 66% with nothing untrue added.

What a client does in 10 minutes

  • Signs in with Google and uploads their resume (PDF or pasted text).
  • Sees their best-matching open jobs near them or remote, with pay when the ad lists it.
  • Picks one, tailors the resume, reviews each change, and downloads a PDF or Word file.
  • Optional: cover letter, answers to application questions, interview prep, and a tracker.

Privacy

  • Each client has their own account. Nothing about a client is shared with you or anyone else.
  • Resumes are never sold or used to train AI models. A client can download or delete everything any time from Settings.

  • Free plan after that: 3 tailored resumes a month. Pro is $15 a month or $29 for 3 months.
