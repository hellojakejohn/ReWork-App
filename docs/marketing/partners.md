# Partners: who to reach first, what to offer, what to send (Nov 22, 2026)

Jake has no warm contacts yet, so partner outreach is the first collab. The edge: Jake lives
in Saint Paul, a short trip from the biggest workforce center in the county. Walking in with a
one-pager beats any cold email.

## The offer (same for everyone)

- **Free for their clients for a month:** a 30-day Pro pass for every client they send. We
  comp it from `/admin`, or give them a partner link. When a partner says yes, Claude adds
  a line to `PARTNERS` in `src/lib/partner-rules.ts` (code, name, 30 days, a cap like 100, an
  end date) and their link is `rework.careers/?ref=<code>`. Each signup through it gets the
  days as a comp, up to the cap; `/admin` shows signups, tailors and payers per code.
- **What they get:** a tool that finds real, open jobs near the client (not ghost jobs),
  tailors the resume to each one without inventing anything, and shows the receipt of every
  change. Counselors can trust the "nothing made up" part, which they can't with ChatGPT.
- **What we ask:** 15 minutes to show it, then their clients try it. After a month, a short
  check-in: how many used it, interviews, any complaints. No client data leaves the client's
  own account.
- **Free tools they can share today, no sign-up:** `/check` (is this job real?), `/fit`
  (resume fit check), `/jobs` (who's hiring by role).

## Walk-in checklist (CareerForce)

- One printed page (Claude builds it in week 2): what it does, the free tools, the offer, a QR
  code to rework.careers/check.
- Ask for: who runs workshops, whether counselors can recommend outside tools, and whether
  a free 30-minute workshop at their site is possible.
- Don't ask for client lists or data. Clients sign up themselves.

## Tracking

One row per partner in the tracker or a simple sheet: contacted, replied, call booked, pilot
started, clients signed up, interviews reported. After 4 weeks, put the numbers in the
growth plan's week 11 review.
