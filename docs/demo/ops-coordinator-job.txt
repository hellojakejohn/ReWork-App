Operations Coordinator
Cedar & Pine Property Management

We're looking for an Operations Coordinator to keep our property management team running smoothly. You'll coordinate vendor schedules, manage work orders, track budgets, and improve internal processes.

What you'll do:
- Schedule and coordinate vendors and maintenance teams
- Track work orders and follow up to resolution
- Maintain inventory of supplies and reorder as needed
- Prepare weekly reports in Excel/Google Sheets
- Support budget tracking and invoice processing
- Improve SOPs and onboarding documentation
- Communicate with residents and handle escalations professionally

What you bring:
- 3+ years in an operations or coordination role
- Strong organization and prioritization
- Proficiency with Excel or Google Sheets
- Vendor management experience
- Customer service and conflict resolution skills
- Experience with scheduling software is a plus
