Scope: the site, the app, matches on upload (PR 47), match accuracy and job supply. Fixes are on
the `matches-on-upload` branch (PR 47) unless noted. Jake's own steps are in `docs/jake-todo.md`.

## How it was checked

  retail manager, new grad, admin assistant) through the real feed (`npm run feed:check --

## Fixed

**Match accuracy**
- "In other words" now reads reordered words ("processed vendor invoices" = Invoice
  Processing), "ran X" = X management, irregular verbs (wrote = writing), Microsoft Office =
  Word/Excel/PowerPoint/Outlook, and ignores filler ("administrative experience"). Real tailor
  check: restaurant manager 36% -> 53%, admin assistant 9% -> 41%, nothing lower, fact guard clean.
- Look-alikes no longer count: "account" isn't Accounting, "go-to-market" isn't Marketing, an end
  date "present" isn't Presentations.
- Job keyword lists skip degrees, years and generic words ("Bachelor's degree", "5+ years",
  "time zones"); "SaaS experience" reads as SaaS.
- The Tailor card now uses the match's own keyword list (by matchId), so its % equals the match
  list's % and the keywords aren't bought twice.
- Each match shows "Tailoring can lift this to N%" when the resume already shows more in other words.

**Which jobs show**
- A job title must share the field word, not just the role word, and the same role when both
  name one: no Recruiting Coordinator for a Logistics Coordinator, no Product Designer for a
  Product Manager or an Instructional Designer. RN reads as nurse; product/UX/UI designer are one field.
- Matches rank by what tailoring can honestly reach plus title fit.
- Pro runs score up to 50 jobs, as the plan promises (they scored 25).
- Searched jobs (Workday, USAJOBS) now close when unseen for 30/14 days, unless someone saved or
  tailored them (they never closed before).

**Pay**
- Ashby's own salary field is used. Perks ("$100/month education budget") and small monthly
  figures aren't pay; "$150,000 plus annual bonus" still is.

**Apply assist and extension** (needs the extension re-uploaded)
- After pressing Apply, the button fills only a real application form (the job's own site, or a
  page with a resume upload and a name/email field), never a job board: Save to ReWork keeps working
  there and the person's email never lands in a job-alert box.
- "Message to the hiring manager" and "Which languages..." questions are no longer blocked by
  the "age" rule.
- The extension's resume fetch logs `apply_resume_attached`, not a download.

**Copy and pages**
- Landing sells matches, answers, interview prep and Apply. Plan lines include matches,
  answers and prep. Privacy and terms cover job matches, answers, interview prep and the
  extension on application forms (dated Nov 14).
- Matches: "near me" with no US state on the resume now says it will show remote jobs and how
  to fix it.

**More jobs**
- USAJOBS (federal) at match time, off until Jake adds the key.
- SmartRecruiters company lists in the daily pull: Equinox, Bosch, Western Digital, Experian,
  Sixt, ServiceNow, NBCUniversal, Avery Dennison, Continental (about 2,400 US jobs).
- 13 more Greenhouse/Lever boards (health care, service, delivery) and 4 more Workday employers.
